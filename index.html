The savings in trees

Your ticket taxes in trees

Most aviation ticket taxes go into the general state budget and are not earmarked for planting trees. This section is a "what if": it shows what the same money would buy if it went to reforestation. Planted trees also absorb slowly, close to nothing in their first years, so they repay a flight over decades, not on the day you fly.
